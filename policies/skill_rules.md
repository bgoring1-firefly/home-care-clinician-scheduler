# Skill Rules

## SR-1 Wound care
Wound care and post-surgical wound dressing require an RN with wound care certification.

## SR-2 Medication teaching
Medication teaching, insulin education, and anticoagulant education require an RN with medication teaching. Diabetes education also requires diabetes education skill.

## SR-3 Physical therapy
Gait training, balance training, fall prevention, hip replacement rehab, and knee replacement rehab require a PT with the matching rehab skill.

## SR-4 Home health aide
Bathing assistance and ADL assistance require a Home Health Aide with ADL or bathing assistance skill.

## SR-5 Speech therapy
Swallowing assessment and dysphagia assessment require a Speech Therapist with dysphagia assessment skill.

## SR-6 Occupational therapy
Home safety evaluation requires an OT with home safety skill.

## SR-7 Behavioral health
Behavioral health medication safety checks require a Behavioral Health Nurse unless the request includes emergency risk language.

## SR-8 Case management and social work
Discharge coordination requires a Case Manager. Community resource assessment requires a Medical Social Worker.

## SR-9 Services not handled by home care
Experimental infusion, inpatient-level monitoring, emergency response, chest pain, severe shortness of breath, and other emergency diagnoses are not handled by routine home care scheduling.

Known Limitations

  • This prototype handles only the new home care visit assignment loop for synthetic patient requests.
  • It expects data in the embedded CSV and policy formats used in this demo; different file shapes would need mapping first.
  • It uses fictional zones, buildings, and estimated travel fit, not real addresses, live maps, or drive-time data.
  • It can recommend and route for review, but it does not finalize schedules or change accepted patient visits.
  • It does not replace clinical judgment for emergency, high-risk, out-of-policy, or ineligible diagnosis cases; those stop for human review.
